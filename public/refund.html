Refund Policy

Last updated: October 2026

We want every payment to end with a clean document in your hands. Here's exactly how refunds work.

1. When you get a refund

We issue a full refund when any of these happen:

  • Your payment went through but the clean PDF never downloaded and we can't fix it for you.
  • You were charged more than once for the same document in the same editing session.
  • The document you received is broken, empty, or materially different from the free preview you approved.

2. When refunds don't apply

  • The PDF downloaded fine — you just changed your mind about the wording, template, or details. In that case, simply edit and preview again free; you only pay once per clean download.
  • Your document was rejected by an employer, school, or other institution. The document is a tool you control; we can't guarantee outcomes (see our Terms).
  • You can't find the file — signed-in users can re-download free from the Document Locker anytime. If you paid as a guest, download your PDF when it's offered and keep a copy; we can regenerate it for you only if you were signed in at the time.

3. How to request one

Email kdocspro@gmail.com with: the phone number or email you paid with, the approximate time of payment, and what went wrong. We take responsibility for fixing genuine payment issues quickly.

4. How and when refunds are sent

Approved refunds are returned through the same channel you paid with (e.g. M-Pesa reversal via our payment provider, IntaSend). We process them within 3 working days of approval; your provider may take a few extra days to reflect the money.

5. Contact

Anything about payments or refunds: kdocspro@gmail.com. See also our Terms and Privacy Policy.
