About KDocs Pro

Documents from your phone, priced for Kenya.

KDocs Pro (also written KDocs-Pro) is a self-service web app that turns a short form into a finished, professional document. You pick the document, choose a template, fill in your details, and get a free watermarked preview straight away. If you like what you see, a clean, watermark-free PDF costs KSh 20 or KSh 50 depending on the document, paid with M-Pesa, card or bank.

It exists because the alternative — a cyber café queue, or wrestling with Word on a borrowed laptop — costs more time than the document is worth.

What you can make

The catalog covers the documents people actually get asked for in Kenya:

  • CVs and résumés and cover letters
  • Job application letters and offer acceptance letters
  • Resignation letters, farewell letters and handover reports
  • Reference requests, recommendation letters and experience certificates
  • Leave requests, school leave permission and salary increment requests
  • Admission applications and fee bursary requests
  • Freelance invoices, meeting minutes and business cards

There are also free PDF tools — merge, split, rotate, and scan paper to PDF — that cost nothing and store nothing.

What you see before you pay

Every document gets a free preview with the watermark removed only after payment, so there are no surprises. The preview also includes Ready Score: a checklist of what makes a document structurally complete (contact details, dates, the sections an employer or school expects), plain-language suggestions for fixing what is missing, and — for CVs — a keyword comparison against a job advert you paste in. It is rule-based: it flags gaps, it never invents details about you.

What it costs

  • Job-hunting documents (CV, cover letter, job application) and admission applications: KSh 50.
  • Most other documents: KSh 20.
  • Previewing, editing, the PDF tools and scanning: free.

The price for a document is always shown on its own page and again at checkout, before you pay.

Payments and your details

Payments are handled by IntaSend, over M-Pesa, card or bank. KDocs Pro never sees or stores your M-Pesa PIN or your full card number. Payments are reconciled with the provider before a clean PDF is released, and signed-in users can re-download documents they have already paid for.

Contact

Questions, refunds or a document that is not working: kdocspro@gmail.com. See also the terms, privacy policy and refund policy.
