Delete your account

Last updated: October 2026

You can delete your KDocs-Pro account yourself, any time, without contacting us. Deleting your account is permanent and cannot be undone.

1. Delete it yourself (fastest)

  • Open kdocs-pro.onrender.com and sign in.
  • Tap Sign in → Profile.
  • Tap Delete account, then tap it again to confirm.

That's it — no email or waiting period.

2. Or ask us by email

If you can't sign in (for example, you lost access to your email), send a message to kdocspro@gmail.com from the email address on the account with the subject "Delete my account" and we will delete it for you.

3. What gets deleted

  • Your profile: name, email, sign-in method.
  • Every document saved in My Documents (your Document Locker).
  • Your sign-in session and the small preferences stored in your browser.

Payment references are not linked to your account; they age out of our payment records automatically within 90 days, as described in our Privacy Policy. See also our Terms and Refund Policy.
