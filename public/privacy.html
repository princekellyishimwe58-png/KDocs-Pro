Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

This policy explains what personal data KDocs-Pro collects, why, how long we keep it, and the choices you have. We designed the service to collect as little as possible: guests can generate and pay for documents without an account.

1. What we collect

  • Document details you type (names, dates, experience, etc.). While you edit, this content is sent securely to our server for one purpose only: to render your live preview and quality check. It is not saved on our servers. It is stored only if you are signed in when you download the document (see the Document Locker below).
  • Contact details you give us at checkout (phone number and/or email) so the payment provider can process your payment and send your receipt.
  • Account details, if you sign up: name, email, sign-in method (email or Google), and your saved documents in the Document Locker.
  • Payment metadata: your payment reference, the document type and amount, provider transaction identifiers, and the payment status (e.g. paid/failed) so your download works reliably and we can resolve payment questions. We never see or store your M-Pesa PIN or full card numbers; those go directly to the payment provider.
  • Basic technical logs (server logs) for security and troubleshooting.

2. Why we process it (and our legal basis)

  • To generate the document you asked for and deliver your PDF — this is the service you requested.
  • To take payments and prevent fraud — necessary for the contract with you and for our legitimate interests.
  • To provide accounts and the Document Locker — with your consent, which you give by creating an account.
  • To keep the service secure — our legitimate interest.

3. Who we share it with

We share only what's necessary with: IntaSend (payment processing — they handle your phone number/email and payment details under their own privacy policy) and Google (if you use "Continue with Google", we receive your basic profile to create/sign in to your account). We also use hosting (Render), a database (MongoDB Atlas), and technical error monitoring (Sentry — it receives error details and page URLs, never your personal details) as service providers. These providers may process data in data centres outside Kenya under their own contractual data-protection safeguards. We do not sell your data, ever.

4. How long we keep it

  • Payment records: up to 90 days, so you can re-download what you paid for and we can resolve payment questions. They are then deleted automatically.
  • Document Locker contents: until you delete them or your account.
  • Server logs: short retention for security purposes.

5. Cookies and browser storage

  • kdocs_csrf — an essential security token that protects forms and payments (lasts 24 hours).
  • kdocs_session — an essential, hidden sign-in cookie, set only if you sign in (lasts 30 days).
  • Kept on your device only: your dark/light theme choice and a temporary draft of the document you are editing, stored in your browser so an accidental reload doesn't lose your work.
  • If you use "Continue with Google", Google's own scripts apply on its sign-in pages (see Google's privacy policy).
  • No advertising or tracking cookies. Ever.

6. Your rights (Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019)

You can ask us to: give you a copy of your data, correct it, delete it, or stop processing it. You can delete your account and saved documents yourself at any time (sign in → Profile → Delete account, or see Delete account). Email kdocspro@gmail.com and we will respond within a reasonable time. You also have the right to complain to the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (ODPC) if you believe your rights were violated.

7. Security

We use HTTPS, hashed passwords (never stored in plain text), signed session cookies, and input validation. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data.

8. Children

The service is intended for people old enough to consent to processing their own data under Kenyan law. If you believe a child's data was shared without proper consent, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Changes and contact

We'll post any changes on this page with an updated date. Questions or data requests: kdocspro@gmail.com.
