      Scan paper & convert to PDF

      Photograph each page with your phone (or pick photos from your gallery), then turn them into one clean PDF. Free — nothing is uploaded or stored, the PDF is built the moment you convert.

      How scanning a paper document works

      • Take one photo per page, or pick them from your gallery - up to 15 pages in one PDF.
      • The page order is the thumbnail order, so remove a page and re-add it if you shot it out of turn.
      • Leave Clean pages ticked for grey photocopies and dim indoor light; it lifts the contrast before the PDF is built.
      • Nothing is uploaded to storage and nothing is kept: the pages are assembled in memory and the finished PDF is returned to your phone straight away.
      • No account and no payment - the scanner is free, whether or not you ever buy a document.

      Common uses: a signed ID copy for a job application, a stamped bank statement, school reports, and a receipt you need to email as one file.

      Need something you can type and edit rather than a photo of paper? Start from a CV, a cover letter or a leave request.

      Free PDF tools

      Turn photos into a PDF, merge PDFs together, extract or delete pages, or fix sideways pages — nothing is stored, your PDF is built and returned immediately.

      🖼️ Photos to PDF

      Turn photos and screenshots (JPG, PNG) into one PDF — ID copies, certificates, receipts, WhatsApp screenshots — one page per photo, in the order you pick them.

      🧩 Merge PDFs

      Combine two or more PDFs into one file in the order you choose them — e.g. CV + cover letter + certificates.

      ✂️ Fix / extract pages

      Remove stray or blank pages, pull out just the pages you need, or straighten pages that were scanned sideways.

      Pages are counted as they appear in the PDF (1, 2, 3…). For rotate, you can leave Pages empty to rotate every page.

      What each PDF tool is for

      • Photos to PDF - one page per photo, for ID copies, certificates, receipts and WhatsApp screenshots that have to arrive as a single file.
      • Merge PDFs - the order you pick the files in is the order they appear, which is what employers expect from a CV followed by certificates.
      • Delete pages - strip blank or duplicate pages before sending a scanned bundle.
      • Keep only these pages - send three relevant pages of a long report instead of all forty.
      • Rotate - straighten a page that came out of a scanner or phone camera sideways; leave the page list empty to rotate the whole document.

      Page numbers are the ones your PDF reader shows, counting from 1, and ranges like 1,3,7-9 are accepted. Every tool runs on the file in memory: nothing is written to storage, and a file limit keeps the tools fast on a phone connection. There is no account, no watermark and no payment.
